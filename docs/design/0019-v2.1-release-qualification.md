## Frozen product scope

Bounded independent text specialists, centralized tool/policy authority,
serialized effects, budgets, lifecycle, cancellation, durable recovery,
status/JSON inspection, SDK APIs and reproducible evaluation are the release
scope. Latent artifact transport, representation APIs and interventions remain
experimental. Space Bunny Alpha cannot provide hidden-state communication.
No latent quality/compute gains, KV transfer, cross-model translation, learned
routing or parallel reasoning are claimed.

## C. Real-model qualification

Existing user settings select `open-ai-compatible`,
`stealth/space-bunny-alpha`, endpoint `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1`, credential
variable `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`. Secrets will not enter protocols or artifacts.
Hosted opaque weights cannot be cryptographically attested. No model fallback.

Engineering and task-quality evidence are separate. Smoke/security runs precede
the immutable scored protocol. Real historical issue graders must reject known
bad states and accept gold fixes. Every declared trial remains in the evidence.
At least three repetitions per arm/task are intended, with equal global budgets
and frozen ordering. Provider failure is recorded rather than replaced.

Protocol, trials, success, tokens, latency, tool/cache usage and failure analysis:
NOT QUALIFIED.
